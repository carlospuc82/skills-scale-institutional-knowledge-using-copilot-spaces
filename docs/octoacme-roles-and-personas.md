# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation. They collaborate with product and engineering to ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and test plans for features and releases
- Create and maintain test cases aligned with acceptance criteria
- Execute manual testing and coordinate automated test coverage
- Identify, document, and triage defects with clear reproduction steps
- Validate acceptance criteria and Definition of Done before PR approval
- Participate in release smoke testing and post-deploy verification
- Recommend quality improvements and process enhancements based on findings

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal defects reaching production
- Reduce time spent on manual regression testing through strategic automation
- Provide clear visibility into feature readiness and quality metrics

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement sessions
- Test result summaries and quality reports in PR reviews
- Defect reports and quality metrics dashboards
- Risk escalation for quality-related blockers

---

## Stakeholders / Sponsors

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors are business owners, executives, or key decision-makers who set strategic priorities, approve project milestones, and provide business context. They have a vested interest in project outcomes and may be internal or external to the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and measurable success metrics
- Approve project charter and authorize release decisions
- Provide funding, budget, and resource allocation decisions
- Attend key milestone reviews and gate decision meetings
- Escalate business blockers and provide executive sponsorship and support
- Communicate business impact, ROI, and strategic alignment
- Manage stakeholder expectations and feedback loops

### Goals
- Ensure project outcomes align with business strategy and objectives
- Minimize project risk and maintain stakeholder confidence and engagement
- Enable timely decision-making by providing clear business context
- Track ROI and demonstrate business value realization

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and milestone reviews
- Project charter approval and strategic gate decisions
- Executive dashboards and risk escalation summaries
- Release announcements and business impact reports

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects own technical direction, system design, and architectural decisions. They guide developers on implementation approaches, ensure scalability and maintainability, and help mitigate technical risks.

### Responsibilities
- Define system architecture and technical approach for major initiatives
- Make key technical decisions and facilitate trade-off analysis discussions
- Conduct technical design reviews and provide architectural guidance
- Mentor developers and promote engineering best practices
- Identify technical debt and recommend refactoring or modernization efforts
- Assess feasibility, scalability, and estimate technical complexity
- Coordinate with other teams on technical integration points and dependencies

### Goals
- Ensure technical solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with long-term vision
- Reduce technical risk and minimize code complexity over time
- Build team technical capability and maintain high code quality standards

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussion forums
- Technical spike documentation and feasibility recommendations
- Code review guidance on major components and architectural concerns
- Technical risk assessment and mitigation plans during planning

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove process blockers, and coach teams on agile practices and continuous improvement. They serve the team and organization in adopting agile ways of working and maximizing team effectiveness.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, sprint reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove impediments and blockers to team progress
- Coach team members on agile practices and self-organization principles
- Track velocity and highlight capacity issues or burnout risks
- Monitor team health, psychological safety, and engagement levels
- Facilitate effective communication and collaboration within and across teams
- Support process improvements identified in retrospectives and action items

### Goals
- Enable high-performing, self-organized, and empowered teams
- Reduce cycle time and improve predictability of delivery
- Create a culture of continuous improvement, learning, and accountability
- Maintain sustainable pace and prevent team burnout

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and regular sprint ceremonies
- Impediment tracking and escalation to unblock teams
- Retrospective facilitation and action item follow-up
- Process improvement recommendations and coaching

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment activities, manage release schedules, and track production health. They ensure releases are well-planned, executed smoothly, and monitored for stability and performance.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and schedule release windows and deployment timelines
- Coordinate pre-release readiness checks and approval gates
- Manage deployment processes, runbooks, and deployment scripts
- Monitor post-deployment health, performance, and user impact
- Manage rollback decisions and incident response procedures
- Communicate release status and manage cross-team dependencies
- Track release metrics and conduct post-release retrospectives

### Goals
- Execute releases with minimal risk and production downtime
- Maintain production stability, performance, and customer experience
- Provide clear visibility into deployment status and readiness
- Continuously improve release processes and reduce cycle time

### Typical Communication
- Pre-release readiness meetings and deployment checklists
- Release notes and deployment schedules shared across teams
- Post-deployment verification reports and health dashboards
- Incident communication and rollback decision notifications

---

## Security Officer / Lead

### Role Summary
Security Officers and Leads ensure security requirements are met throughout the project lifecycle. They conduct security reviews, manage vulnerabilities, and coordinate incident response for security-related issues.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and conduct threat modeling for new features
- Conduct security design and code reviews before deployment
- Manage vulnerability scanning, assessment, and remediation efforts
- Ensure compliance with security policies, standards, and regulations
- Lead security incident investigations and coordinate response procedures
- Recommend security improvements and promote security best practices
- Coordinate with compliance, risk, and legal teams on security matters

### Goals
- Minimize security vulnerabilities and reduce breach risk
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and organizational security standards
- Build security awareness and practices into team culture and processes
- Enable fast incident detection, response, and recovery

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements documentation in planning and design phases
- Code review comments highlighting security concerns and recommendations
- Vulnerability reports and remediation tracking dashboards
- Security incident escalation and post-incident review communications

---

## Design / UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design and UX Leads own user experience strategy, create design specifications, and validate usability. They ensure features are intuitive, accessible, and meet user needs while supporting business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and define user personas and customer journey maps
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and detailed design specifications
- Validate design assumptions through user testing and feedback collection
- Ensure accessibility compliance and design system consistency
- Collaborate with product and engineering teams on feasibility and tradeoffs
- Review implementations against design specifications for fidelity
- Recommend UX improvements based on user metrics and feedback

### Goals
- Create intuitive, delightful user experiences that drive adoption
- Reduce user friction and support successful feature adoption
- Build consistent, scalable design systems and reusable patterns
- Inform product decisions with user research insights and metrics

### Typical Communication
- Design specifications and design system documentation
- User research findings and usability test result summaries
- Design reviews and implementation validation meetings
- UX metrics, analytics, and user feedback analysis reports

---

## Cross-Persona Interactions

These personas work together throughout the OctoAcme project lifecycle:

- **Initiation**: Stakeholders, Product Manager, and Project Manager align on business case and priorities
- **Planning**: QA Lead, Technical Lead, Security Officer, and Design Lead contribute to scope definition and risk assessment
- **Execution**: Developers, QA Lead, Technical Lead, Scrum Master, and Design Lead collaborate in daily ceremonies and reviews
- **Release**: Release Manager, QA Lead, Security Officer, and Developers coordinate pre-release, deployment, and post-deploy verification
- **Retrospective**: Scrum Master facilitates; all personas contribute learnings and identify improvement opportunities
